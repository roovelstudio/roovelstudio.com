Our approach

Ship small. Talk early. Keep going.

We don't have a big team or a big budget, so we spend both carefully — picking ideas with a tight core, prototyping fast, and putting them in front of real players long before launch.

Coming from QA, dev, and art, we sweat the details other small studios skip: localization into 15 languages on day one, store pages that actually convert, and systems that hold up under players who push them hard.

Who you're writing about

The name is two words pushed together.

Roovel comes from root and level. The root is the part we never planned — four people circling each other for years across different cities, different studios and different job titles, never quite drifting apart. The level is what those years added: QA, design, production, art, all of it accumulating separately until we looked up and realised we had a whole team.

So in January 2026 we came back to Denizli, the one place all four of us started from, and got to work. The full version of this story is in the kit.

Privacy policy

This site collects as little as possible. Here is exactly what happens when you visit it, in plain language.

Last updated: August 2026

Who we are

Roovel Studio is an independent game studio based in Denizli, Türkiye. This policy covers roovelstudio.com only — not our games, and not third-party platforms such as Steam, Discord or any social network, which have their own policies.

What we collect

We do not use tracking cookies, we do not build advertising profiles, and we never sell or rent your data. There are only three ways any information reaches us:

The contact form. When you send us a message we receive your name, email address, chosen topic and message text. We use it to reply to you and nothing else. Submissions are delivered by Web3Forms, which processes the message on our behalf.

The mailing list. If you sign up we store your email address so we can tell you when a game launches, a playtest opens or a new title joins the lineup. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to remove you at any time.

Anonymous analytics. We use Umami Analytics to see how many people visit, which pages they read and which outbound links they click. Umami does not use cookies, does not store IP addresses and cannot identify you or follow you across other websites. The statistics we see are aggregate counts, never individual visitors.

Services this site loads

Typefaces are served by Google Fonts, and the site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. Both receive your IP address as a technical necessity of delivering the page, and both keep short-lived server logs. We have no access to those logs.

How long we keep things

Contact messages are kept while the conversation is useful and deleted once it clearly isn't. Mailing list addresses are kept until you unsubscribe. Analytics data holds no personal information at any point.

Your rights

Under Turkish data protection law (KVKK) and the GDPR you can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to us and we will sort it out — no forms, no hoops.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has sent us something, tell us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about any of this? Email info@roovelstudio.com or use the contact page.
